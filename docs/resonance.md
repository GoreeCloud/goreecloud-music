# GoreeCloud Resonance

## Identity

**GoreeCloud Resonance** is the official umbrella capability identity for the complete first-party feature system delivered through **GoreeCloud Music**.

GoreeCloud Music remains the application, service, music-domain authority, and runtime boundary. Resonance names and organizes durable music capability domains; it is not a separate daemon, database, account system, permission authority, or deployment boundary.

Approved hierarchy:

`GoreeCloud Suite → GoreeCloud Music → GoreeCloud Resonance → Resonance capability families`

Preferred presentation:

**GoreeCloud Music — Powered by Resonance.**

## Capability families

- **Resonance Library** — multi-user libraries, artists, albums, tracks, genres, composers, favorites, ratings, recent activity, permissions, and metadata-driven browsing.
- **Resonance Audio** — original-quality and lossless streaming, adaptive transcoding, codecs, normalization, gapless playback, crossfade, buffering, quality profiles, and technical playback information.
- **Resonance Player** — Now Playing, queues, shuffle, repeat, playback state, mini-player behavior, volume, device controls, and session continuity.
- **Resonance Discover** — Home, Explore, personalized rows, resurfacing, genre/mood/decade exploration, and locally derived recommendations.
- **Resonance Mixes** — Daily Mixes, Discovery, Favorites, Throwback, Chill, Workout, Forgotten Favorites, Deep Cuts, Recently Rediscovered, Unplayed Albums, and future generated mixes.
- **Resonance Radio** — song, artist, album, genre, playlist, mood, library, and family radio with dynamically extended queues.
- **Resonance Playlists** — personal, shared, collaborative, family, smart, imported, exported, and optionally link-shared playlists with authorization-preserving collaboration.
- **Resonance Lyrics** — embedded, plain-text, synchronized `.lrc`, future word-level sync, translation, transliteration/pronunciation, and karaoke presentation.
- **Resonance Credits** — rich music, release, contributor, artwork, and technical metadata.
- **Resonance Offline** — native downloads, offline playback, configurable offline quality, and future Smart Downloads.
- **Resonance Search** — music-domain search and future GoreeCloud Search interoperability.
- **Resonance Together** — household sharing, collaborative experiences, attributable shared activity, and permission-aware multi-user features.
- **Resonance Profile** — per-user favorites, ratings, history, recommendations, queues, devices, playback preferences, downloads, and personalization state.
- **Resonance Intelligence** — local recommendation models, affinity signals, sequence analysis, optional local audio analysis, and privacy-preserving first-party music intelligence.
- **Resonance Connect** — native APIs, OpenSubsonic compatibility, approved external clients, playback-device integration, migration interfaces, and future automotive/TV/casting interoperability.
- **Resonance Import** — filesystem scanning, metadata extraction, artwork ingestion, playlist import, and migration from compatible music systems.
- **Resonance Preserve** — portable exports, state preservation, library independence, migration support, and Everkeep integration.

## Naming rule

Resonance is an umbrella capability identity, not a requirement to replace familiar music terms. The UI may continue to use clear nouns such as Home, Explore, Search, Library, Artists, Albums, Songs, Playlists, Lyrics, Queue, Downloads, Favorites, Settings, and Now Playing whenever those labels are clearest.

## Platform identity boundaries

- **Glaze UI** remains the GoreeCloud design language.
- **Wardveil Security** remains the platform security identity.
- **GoreeCloud Privacy Shield** remains the privacy identity.
- **Everkeep** remains the resilience, backup, recovery, preservation, and digital-legacy identity.
- **GoreeCloud Suite** remains the integrated application ecosystem.
- **GoreeCloud Music** remains the music application and runtime authority.

Resonance must complement these identities rather than replace or dilute them.

## Privacy and implementation boundaries

Resonance Intelligence is local and privacy-preserving by default. External metadata, lyrics, scrobbling, artwork, recommendation, or discovery providers must remain optional and explicitly controlled.

A Resonance family name does not imply implementation completion, runtime acceptance, release readiness, or production approval. Each capability remains subject to GoreeCloud security, privacy, accessibility, testing, backup, recovery, release-lifecycle, interoperability, and production-readiness requirements.

## Visual direction

A future Resonance visual identity should communicate resonance, waveform interaction, harmonic relationship, propagation, connection, or layered musical energy without becoming a generic music-note, equalizer, headphone, or play-button icon. No canonical Resonance mark is approved by this source document alone.
