# GoreeCloud Music — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Music should eventually combine the strengths expected from mature self-hosted music servers and modern music clients while preserving GoreeCloud's own architecture, privacy model, and design identity.

## Match or exceed

- Reliable self-hosted library scanning, metadata, search, and playback.
- High-quality/lossless playback and transparent quality handling.
- Strong playlist, queue, offline, lyrics, recommendation, radio, and multi-device experiences.
- Native Android, web, and Linux delivery with appropriate additional platforms over time.
- OpenSubsonic interoperability without letting compatibility dictate internal architecture.

## Implement differently

- Source identity and availability remain separate first-class metadata.
- External providers are optional adapters, not hidden substitutes for private library content.
- Private recommendations and listening analytics default to local/user-scoped processing.
- Platform-System security, privacy, recovery, identity, management, coordination, and Glaze UI contracts are explicit release gates.

## Reject

- Advertising identifiers or mandatory behavioral tracking.
- Silent replacement of one recording with a different version merely because metadata looks similar.
- Mandatory third-party control planes for core self-hosted library operation.
- Treating a branded upstream fork as permanent native GoreeCloud implementation.
