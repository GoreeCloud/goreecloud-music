# GoreeCloud Music — Benefits

GoreeCloud Music is intended to provide a private, durable and user-controlled music experience without making a third-party catalog provider the permanent center of the product.

## User benefits

- **Ownership and independence:** user-owned libraries remain useful without a commercial streaming subscription.
- **Privacy:** listening history, search, recommendations and analytics can remain local or GoreeCloud-controlled by default.
- **Source transparency:** users can see where media comes from and whether it is locally available.
- **Reliable offline use:** authorized downloads and cached assets are designed as first-class states rather than ambiguous provider behavior.
- **Recording fidelity:** routing is designed around recording identity so remasters, live versions, edits and covers are not silently substituted.
- **Multi-user isolation:** profiles, histories, recommendations, queues, downloads and permissions remain separate unless explicitly shared.
- **Interoperability:** OpenSubsonic-compatible access and migration paths can coexist with a native GoreeCloud API and data model.
- **Cross-device continuity:** planned web, desktop, mobile, automotive and living-room experiences share product semantics without collapsing device authorization boundaries.

## Platform benefits

- **Glaze UI consistency:** GoreeCloud Music participates in GoreeCloud’s shared visual and interaction language while preserving music-specific workflows.
- **Wardveil integration:** security, sessions, authorization, sharing and download controls can use common GoreeCloud security contracts.
- **Privacy Shield integration:** provider access and sensitive listening operations can carry explicit privacy authorization and purpose boundaries.
- **Everkeep integration:** application state, metadata and recovery information can be protected without making the application database the sole copy of original media.
- **GoreeCloud Mesh and Manager integration:** health, dependencies, capabilities and operational state can participate in platform coordination without exposing private listening content by default.

These are product objectives. They do not establish current implementation, production acceptance or Stable status.
